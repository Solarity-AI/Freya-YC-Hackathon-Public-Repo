Facet F6: read-back, recording, required sentences, closing and canned lines.

- confirmation: readback (the read-back sentence with <items>, e.g. "So that is <items>."), readback_item (one
  item, e.g. "<amount> for your <product> on <date>"), question (the confirmation question exactly as the brief
  words it), verbatim_question (true when the brief gives the exact question), on_change
  "revalidate_then_readback", what_to_change (asked after a bare no), recorded_cannot_change (said when the customer
  asks to change a promise that is already recorded, as the brief says).
- recording: tries (1 + the retries the brief allows for a failed create, e.g. "retry once" = 2), recorded
  (said after a successful create), recorded_multi (for several promises), failed (said when all tries failed),
  failed_item (said per product whose promise failed while others were recorded; uses <product>),
  on_all_failed (the situation then, e.g. S_SERVICE_ERROR).
- required_sentences: every sentence the brief says must be said, word for word: id (snake_case), text (exactly as
  the brief writes it, verbatim true), trigger (after_promise_recorded | after_verified | after_statement |
  before_close | on_situation:S_X | on_intent:<intent id> | on_outcome:<outcome>), once (true unless repeated).
- closing.goodbye: thanks + goodbye (always the last sentence before the call is closed). per_situation: extra
  lines for specific situations (situation id -> line), {} if none. no_goodbye: situations for which the brief
  says the agent says its one line and nothing else (e.g. only condolences when the customer has died): no
  closing.goodbye is added after it; [] if none.
- A number the brief writes twice ("two (2) business days") is spoken once ("two business days").
- lines: canned runtime lines; give a key only when the brief words it differently from the generic default.
