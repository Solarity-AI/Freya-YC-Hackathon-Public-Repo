Facet F4: promise dates, amounts and the persuasion ladder.

- promise_date: earliest (DateRule, usually today), latest ({"kind": "deadline"}), business_day_only,
  non_business_within_deadline ("offer_next_business_day" when a weekend/holiday before the deadline gets the next
  business day offered), after_deadline ("offer_deadline"), offered_date_refused ("flat_refusal"), vague
  (ask_exact_times: how often to ask for an exact day after a vague period; then "propose_deadline"),
  past_date ("say_passed_and_ask").
- amounts: first (the amount of the first ask, usually {"kind": "full"}); never_ask_customer (true when the agent
  must never ask how much the customer can pay); too_much (what to do when the customer says the full amount is too
  much: offer (AmountRule, e.g. floor), times (how often), date ("attempt_1" = the floor is offered with the date of
  attempt 1; use "attempt_1" unless the brief explicitly ties that floor offer to another date), say_rest (the sentence about the remainder, as the brief says), refusal "flat_refusal");
  accept_min / accept_max (the range a customer-proposed amount is accepted in, e.g. floor .. full);
  below_floor (say: ONE statement for an amount below the floor, never a question, because the code adds the floor
  offer question itself; may use <floor> and <amount> (= the customer's amount); offer; times; then "flat_refusal");
  above_full "offer_full"; decimals 2.
- negotiation.first_ask: single (the first ask for one product, as the brief words it), multi (the combined first
  ask when there are several products), amount (AmountRule), date (DateRule, usually today), consumes_attempt
  (false: the first ask is not a persuasion attempt unless the brief says so).
- negotiation.ladder: the persuasion attempts in order, one rung per attempt: key ("r1", "r2", ...), attempt
  (1, 2, ...), bands (["all"] or the band keys it applies to), amount (AmountRule; "the full amount, or the floor
  if the customer already said the full amount is too much" = {"kind": "floor_if_too_much_else_full"}), date
  (DateRule), line (the proposal, e.g. "Could you pay <amount> by <date>?"). The number of rungs that apply to a
  band must equal that band's attempts.
- no_repeat_refused_offer: true when an offer the customer refused must not be repeated.
  non_answer_counts_as_refusal: true when a non-answer uses an attempt. on_exhausted: the situation when attempts
  are used up (S_REFUSED_EXHAUSTED). forbidden_offers: short names of the offers never to make
  (one to three words each); never a rule sentence.
