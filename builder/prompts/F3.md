Facet F3: products, the debt statement, the calendar and the bands. (products.fields is bound by code: omit it.)

- products.max: the most products one customer can have in this brief (1 if the brief says one product; if the
  brief is silent, 3). products.types: one entry per product type enum value of the account tool that the brief
  describes, keyed by the enum value: spoken (how to name it, e.g. "<type words> ending <f:LEAF>" when the brief
  names a last-digits leaf of the product), floor (AmountRule; e.g. {"kind": "field", "field": "<leaf>"} when the
  brief names a product leaf as the minimum acceptable amount; {"kind": "full"} when the brief allows no less than
  the full amount; percent_of_full with pct when it is a percentage). floor_missing: "full".
  unknown_type_spoken: a neutral word for a type the brief does not name ("account").
- statement.intro: said once right after verification, before the products (who is calling and why, as the brief
  says). statement.item: one sentence per product using <product>, <past_due>, <days_late>.
- calendar: business_weekdays (Python weekday numbers, Monday = 0), holidays (YYYY-MM-DD as listed), holiday_rules
  (rules for named/recurring holidays), week_start (0 = Monday unless the brief defines the week otherwise),
  count_from ("after_today": the n-th business day after today never counts today), numeric_order (how the brief
  and customers write numeric dates), this_next ("this <weekday>" = current_week; "next <weekday>" =
  following_week when the brief says next means the following calendar week).
  half_days: the brief's half days (e.g. the eve of a holiday whose afternoon is off), one {date, from} each:
  from = "HH:MM", the first time no longer allowed that day (a callback at or after it is refused; "only until
  11:45" -> "11:46"). Omit half_days when the brief has none. half_day_is_business_day: false only when the brief
  says a half day is not a business day for promise dates; else true.
- bands: one per row of the brief's lateness table: key (the brief's band name, snake_case), days_late_min,
  days_late_max (inclusive integers), deadline (DateRule: the latest promise date), attempts (the number of
  persuasion attempts for that band). Quote every row.
